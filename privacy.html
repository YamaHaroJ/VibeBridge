Privacy & data practices

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how VibeBridge handles information when you visit its website or use its macOS agent, Chrome extension, and project-context features (“VibeBridge,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). Read it together with any notices shown in the version you install.

Important scope note: VibeBridge is an evolving product. This policy describes the intended and currently documented architecture; exact data flows can vary by release and by how you configure or use connected services. Do not put passwords, API keys, access tokens, insurance/customer records, or other sensitive personal information in project notes or prompts unless you have independently assessed the risks and necessity.

1. At a glance

  • VibeBridge is designed to use project files, Git metadata, and project notes to help prepare context for a development conversation.
  • The local agent is designed to run on your Mac and bind to loopback rather than expose its local service directly to the public internet.
  • VibeBridge does not access private history from your other ChatGPT conversations as a source of project memory.
  • Information you choose to include in a prompt may be sent to the AI service or website you use to submit that prompt, under that provider’s terms and privacy policy.
  • The public website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request data under its own policies.
  • VibeBridge does not promise that all data remains exclusively on your device in every configuration. Review the behavior and permissions of your installed version and connected services.

2. Information VibeBridge may handle

2.1 Project and repository information

When you select or reference a project, VibeBridge may read or process relevant repository information such as project names and paths, README files, Git branch and commit metadata, working-tree status, and other project files needed for a requested operation. The specific files depend on the feature used and permissions granted.

2.2 Saved project memory

If you save project notes, VibeBridge may store durable decisions, instructions, constraints, blockers, and next steps so they can be included in later context. Treat saved notes as project data, not as a secure secrets vault. Avoid saving credentials, customer records, private keys, or information you are not authorized to process.

2.3 Prompts and context you submit

When you use an extension feature to assemble or insert context, that context may become part of the prompt you submit to ChatGPT or another service. That service receives and processes the information according to its own settings and policies. VibeBridge does not control the provider’s retention, training, or handling practices.

2.4 Local agent and extension technical data

The local agent and browser extension may process connection state, selected project identifiers, local paths, request metadata, diagnostic messages, and authentication material needed to communicate securely with the local agent. A local bearer token is a security credential: do not share it or include it in screenshots, logs, or project notes. Exact diagnostics and retention depend on the installed version.

2.5 Website and support communications

The website host may process technical information such as IP address, request time, browser information, and requested URL in server or security logs. If you contact the operator, the information you send—including your email address and message contents—may be used to respond and maintain support records.

2.6 Information we do not intentionally seek

VibeBridge is not designed to collect private history from unrelated ChatGPT conversations, and it should not require you to provide sensitive personal information to use the website. Do not assume that a project folder is safe to process merely because it is local; repositories can contain secrets or personal data.

3. How information may be used

Information may be processed to:

  • identify the selected project and assemble relevant project context;
  • preserve notes and decisions that you explicitly choose to save;
  • perform local operations that you request and authorize in supported versions;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, protect, and improve the software;
  • respond to support inquiries and communicate important product or security information; and
  • meet legal obligations or investigate misuse, security incidents, or threats to users and systems.

We do not describe project content as being used for advertising or sold to data brokers. This statement is not a guarantee about third-party services you choose to connect; their practices are governed by their own terms.

4. When information may be shared

Information may be disclosed in these circumstances:

  • Services you choose: when you submit a prompt, use an integration, or otherwise direct information to a third-party service, that service processes the information you send.
  • Hosting and infrastructure: website hosting, source distribution, and infrastructure providers may process technical information needed to deliver and secure their services.
  • At your direction: when you export, copy, publish, or otherwise share project content.
  • Legal and safety reasons: where disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect rights or safety, or investigate abuse or security incidents.
  • Business changes: if the product or its assets are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale, information may be transferred subject to applicable requirements and appropriate safeguards.

VibeBridge is not intended to transmit your entire repository automatically to the website operator. The amount of context used depends on the feature and what you request. Verify the actual behavior of your build before using it with confidential material.

5. Storage, retention, and security

5.1 Where information is stored

Project files remain in their existing locations unless a feature you use writes or exports data elsewhere. Project memory may be stored locally or in the configured project-memory location. Prompt content can be stored by the AI provider or other service to which you submit it. Website request logs are controlled by the hosting provider. Exact storage locations and retention are implementation- and configuration-dependent.

5.2 Retention and deletion

Local project files and notes generally remain until you or the relevant application removes them. Copies in third-party services, backups, logs, or records may persist according to those providers’ retention practices. Removing a note locally does not delete copies already submitted to an AI service, committed to version control, shared with others, or included in backups. Before deleting project data, consider repository history and backups as well as the current working copy.

5.3 Security safeguards and your responsibilities

The project is designed with local-first operation and loopback-bound agent access. No software, network transmission, or storage method can be guaranteed perfectly secure. Keep the agent and extension updated, protect your Mac account, use only trusted extensions, safeguard local tokens, limit filesystem and command permissions, and review commands before authorizing them. Do not expose the local agent to a public interface or disable its authentication.

Where a security incident affects information under the operator’s control, the operator will assess the incident and take steps required by applicable law. This policy does not promise that every incident can be prevented or that every third-party incident is under the operator’s control.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Project selection: choose which repository or project to work with and avoid including unrelated files.
  • Prompt review: inspect generated context before submitting it to an AI service.
  • Saved notes: review and remove project notes you no longer want to retain, where the installed version supports that action.
  • Permissions: revoke browser or operating-system permissions you no longer want to grant, understanding that some features may stop working.
  • Third-party settings: manage history, deletion, and privacy settings directly with the AI or hosting provider involved.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about certain personal data, or to object to or restrict some processing. These rights vary by jurisdiction and may be subject to exceptions. Contact the operator to make a request; we may need enough information to verify and respond to it. Data held solely by a third-party provider may need to be requested from that provider.

VibeBridge is a developer tool, not a data-hosting or records-management system. You are responsible for confirming that you have permission to process any third-party, employer, client, or customer information included in a project.

7. International processing and third-party services

Hosting and AI providers may process information in countries other than your own. Those providers’ terms, security practices, and transfer mechanisms govern their processing. Review their privacy documentation before sending confidential or regulated information. VibeBridge cannot make representations on behalf of independent providers.

8. Children

VibeBridge is a developer-oriented tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the operator, contact us so the situation can be reviewed and appropriate action taken.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as VibeBridge’s features, infrastructure, or legal obligations change. The “Last updated” date at the top identifies the most recent revision. Material changes should be reflected on this page. Your continued use after an update is subject to the revised policy to the extent permitted by applicable law.

10. Contact and policy limitations

For privacy requests, questions, or concerns, contact the VibeBridge project operator through the official contact channel provided with the software or on the product website. Before public commercial launch, the operator should publish a monitored privacy contact address and identify the responsible legal entity or business name here.

This page is intended to explain product data practices in plain language; it is not legal advice and does not create a warranty or override applicable law. Because the product is evolving, the operator should review this policy against the actual code, telemetry, deployment configuration, and third-party services before relying on it as a final legal document.
